Continuing and Downloading are the same blue

Measured on the running build: of every pair on the ladder these two are the closest — ΔE 49 in Lab, hues 23° apart, both bright and both cool. At the 16×4 strip in the chip row and the 5px bar on a poster they read as one colour. Downloading cannot move — it is the app's primary blue — so Continuing moves.

Each treatment shows the chip row, a wall of posters, and the two colours touching with nothing else on screen, in both themes. A teal that separates in the dark can collapse into the page in the light, so both have to be judged.
