Where the state goes, and where the count goes

Five treatments on the real shelf, drawn with Deluno's own tokens and the real artwork off this box. Every one keeps the subtitle bar on the bottom edge. What changes is whether the top bar carries a word, and where the episode count lives.

Missing Upgradable Quality met Continuing

Now — what is on the shelf today, with the Quality switch on

Bar grows to carry the tier when the Quality switch is on. The word sits over the dimmed track, which is the wash-out. Count is nowhere on the poster.

Recommended · A — Bar says the state. Corner says the count. No words on artwork.

The bar drops the label entirely and goes back to a 5px strip, filled to how far through you are. The count moves to the top-right corner, where the dot used to be, in a dark pill with the state's colour as a pip. Quality leaves the poster — it is already on the list row, in the drawer and on the detail page. Nothing sits on the bar, so there is nothing to wash out, ever.

A film has no fraction, so its corner carries the state word instead of a count — one pill, always in the same place, always saying the most specific thing it can.

B — A, but quality stays on the artwork

Same bar and same corner, with the tier as a small dark badge bottom-left. Keeps the fact on the poster without ever putting it on the bar. Costs a fourth thing to read on a 150px card.

C — No bar at all. One pill in the corner does both.

The state becomes the pill's own colour and the count sits on it. The top edge goes back to being artwork. Cleanest card here, and the weakest at a glance across a wall — a 20px pill in a corner is far less scannable than a full-width edge, which is the whole reason the dot became a bar.

D — Bar says the state at full strength. Corner says the progress.

The bar is solid — no dim track, no fill fraction, just the colour — and all the "how far along" lives in the corner pill. The two facts stop sharing one control. Loses the at-a-glance sense of progress across a shelf, which the fill gives you without reading anything.
