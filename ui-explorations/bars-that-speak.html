Deeper colours, and bars that say what they are

Two decisions, kept apart so neither hides the other. First how deep the ladder goes — every rung, not just Continuing. Second what the bars carry and how a reader knows the top one is about the media and the bottom one is about subtitles.

Sonarr and Radarr both call this “Detailed Progress Bar — show text on progress bar”. Sonarr writes 16 / 16, Radarr writes Remux‑2160p. Neither washes out, and the reason is not the colour they picked: they draw the label twice — a muted copy on the empty track and a bright copy clipped to exactly the fill width. Every part of the word is coloured for the ground directly under it. Every bar below uses that.
