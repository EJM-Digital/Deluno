James: "monitoring should be something that takes on the word monitor and unmonitor dont you think? or does it not matter?" — it matters, and the answer has a shape: a state word describes, a verb instructs. Anything that merely shows the fact wants Monitored / Not monitored; anything you press wants Monitor / Unmonitor, because a button should say what pressing it does.

Sonarr does both at once. Its control is an icon whose tooltip reads “Monitored, click to unmonitor” and “Unmonitored, click to monitor” — read from its own DOM, not recalled. State first, action second, one control.

Deluno currently says it seven ways. State as Monitored / Not monitored (poster, overview), Monitored / Unmonitored (bulk dialog), On / Paused (movie stats) and Monitoring paused (movie eyebrow); action as Monitor / Unmonitor (selection bar), Monitor / Stop monitoring (movie button) and Monitor or unmonitor (bulk dropdown). Whichever option below wins, it wins everywhere — that is the point of picking one.
