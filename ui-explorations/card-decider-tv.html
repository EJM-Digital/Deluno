The TV card ← Movie shelf

A show is many files. The reader's question is how far through am I, and is more coming? A show has no single quality — twenty episodes can sit at twenty different tiers, and printing any one of them would be picking an arbitrary file and calling it the show. That is why this card cannot say what the movie card says, and why Sonarr prints 16 / 16 where Radarr prints Remux‑2160p.

The show cardsays
Top bar — textAired episodes held: 3 / 20. When nothing has aired, the state's own word — Upcoming.
Top bar — fillCoverage — the fraction of what has aired that is on disk. Counted over what has aired and never over what will exist: an ongoing show measured against its eventual episode count reads permanently unfinished, which is true of every ongoing show and so tells you nothing.
Bottom barSUBS 2 / 6 — summed across the episodes you actually hold. A show short of episodes already says so on the bar above, in red, on the same card; dragging this one down for the same reason would be the same fact twice.
Only hereContinuing — you have every episode that has aired, and more are still to come. The one rung the movie shelf does not have, and the only colour choice left open for review.
Also goneThe corner pill, and any bar under the poster rather than on it.

Implemented recommendation: reuse the movie colours wherever the state matches. The held portion is green, a fully held title at the requested quality is gold, and the remainder is Missing red. Unmonitored always overrides the composition with one flat grey. Continuing remains a TV-only state in the legend, while its coverage still follows the held/quality rule.
